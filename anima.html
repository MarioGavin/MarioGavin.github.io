Anima

Game Designer, Level Designer & Narrative Co-Writer

Anima - Official Gameplay Trailer.

Anima is a story-driven 2D platformer set in the fictional world of Yalos, ruled by six powerful Gods. The project won "Best Elevator Pitch" at the Universidad San Jorge GotY Gala. Working in a team of 5, my primary responsibility was designing the core systemic mechanics, crafting the level flow, and ensuring the narrative deeply intertwined with the gameplay.

Play Game on Itch.io

Core Design: The Aura Mechanic

I wanted to create a mechanic that physically represented the protagonist's (Hylah, Goddess of Feelings) internal struggle. I conceptualized and designed The Aura, a dual-world system that acts as the foundational pillar for combat, exploration, and health management.

  • Ludonarrative Health System: The Aura acts as Hylah's health bar. Every time she takes damage, her dark Aura expands. If the darkness covers the entire screen, the player dies. Healing (collecting animas) shrinks the Aura back down.
  • Dynamic Environment & Hidden Paths: Outside the Aura, the world is bright and peaceful. Inside the Aura, the world's dark truth is revealed. I designed the levels so that taking damage (expanding the Aura) is sometimes necessary to reveal hidden platforms, secret skull totems required to open doors, and deadly traps.
  • Systemic Enemy Behavior: I designed the enemy AI logic to react to the Aura. Creatures like the Wingaz Bird or the Gorod Rocks are entirely peaceful in the light. However, if the player's expanding Aura touches them, they become corrupted and instantly transition into aggressive attack patterns.
"The Aura creates a constant risk-reward loop: sustaining damage grants the player a wider field of view for puzzle-solving and platforming, but pushes them dangerously close to death and triggers nearby enemies."

Dynamic Aura: Revealing hidden platforms and environmental storytelling.

Level & Encounter Design

I structured the game into three distinct pacing phases to keep the gameplay loop engaging:

  • Progress Levels: Focus on traversal, platforming, and managing the Aura to avoid turning peaceful creatures into hostile threats.
  • Puzzle Levels (Time-Attack): I designed specific puzzle rooms where the Aura automatically grows over time. The player must race against the clock to find color-coded blocks (Yellow, Blue, Red) and place them on their corresponding platforms before the darkness consumes the screen.
  • Boss Arenas: Designed the spatial layouts and encounter flow for the multi-phase battles against the other Gods, such as Serfe, God of Elements, who attacks with falling stalactites and defensive crystal walls.

Lore & Worldbuilding

I co-wrote the narrative foundation to support the gameplay. The overarching goal is to liberate Yalos from the oppressive Gods (Body, Elements, Time, Nature, Reason). By making Hylah the Goddess of Feelings, the mechanic of the Aura directly reflects her journey: balancing sadness and hope to overcome physical obstacles.
